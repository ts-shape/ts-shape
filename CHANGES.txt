add: PowerIntegrator (ts_shape.transform.calculator.power_integration) --
  the missing kW -> kWh step. The events.energy detectors aggregate their
  input with sum(), i.e. they assume energy deltas in kWh; feeding an
  instantaneous active-power tag (e.g. *.Total_Active_Power-kW) in directly

  energy pack can consume it; segment_energy exposes the per-sample audit
