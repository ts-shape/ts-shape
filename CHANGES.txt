# August 20, 2026
add: PowerIntegrator (ts_shape.transform.calculator.power_integration) --
  the missing kW -> kWh step. The events.energy detectors aggregate their
  input with sum(), i.e. they assume energy deltas in kWh; feeding an
  instantaneous active-power tag (e.g. *.Total_Active_Power-kW) in directly
  produced a number that scaled with the logging rate rather than with
  consumption, and a plain mean() was a sample-count average that over-weighted
  bursts. PowerIntegrator.energy_by_window integrates properly -- each sample
  weighted by how long it was valid, segments split at window boundaries
  (a hold from 00:55 to 01:05 books 5 minutes to each hour), locf or linear
  interpolation, max_gap to drop logging outages instead of billing phantom
  energy, power_unit for W/kW/MW tags, and per-signal integration before
  summing (energy adds across machines, power does not). Reports coverage per
  window and omits unmeasured windows rather than reporting them as 0 kWh.
  to_energy_deltas emits the result in the standard ts-shape schema so the
  energy pack can consume it; segment_energy exposes the per-sample audit
  trail. Exported at the top level as ts_shape.PowerIntegrator.

# August 10, 2026
add: AssetHealthIndexEvents (events.maintenance.asset_health_index) -- fuses
  several degradation signals into one weighted 0-100 asset health index using
  the same drift/variance/trend penalty model as
  DegradationDetectionEvents.health_score. health_index reports the dominant
  failure mode and a confidence level (share of configured signal weight that
  had data); health_events emits warning/critical crossings; signal_contributions
  ranks the signals by the health they cost the asset; maintenance_forecast
  extrapolates to a critical threshold and withholds a date when the trend fit
  is weaker than min_confidence. Closes gap 3.1 of the manufacturing-operations
  research doc.
add: ProductionScheduleAdherenceEvents (events.production.schedule_adherence) --
  joins a planned schedule (start/end/planned_qty/order_id) against actual
  production from a monotonic counter. adherence_by_period reports adherence %,
  deviation units, an ahead/on_track/behind/critical_behind band, and the
  units/hour needed to recover in the time left in the period; deviation_events
  emits the breaches; adherence_summary rolls the horizon up weighted by planned
  quantity. Actuals are reset-aware by default (handle_resets=True).
add: RootCauseCorrelationEvents (events.correlation.root_cause_correlation) --
  directed, event-driven counterpart to AnomalyCorrelationEvents: takes another
  detector's events and ranks candidate signals by lagged correlation with the
  event onsets. Only non-negative lags are searched (a cause precedes its
  effect) and the winning lag's p-value is Bonferroni-corrected for the lag
  scan, so noise scanned over many lags is not promoted to a root cause.
  rank_causes / top_cause / lag_profile.

add: OEECalculator.calculate_oee now threads handle_resets to its performance
  and quality components and max_gap to its availability/performance components.
  Previously the top-level OEE could not benefit from reset handling, so a day
  with a counter reset silently dragged OEE toward zero; and availability booked

  runtime_per_window, and operating_hours_meter -- a gap to the next sample

  longer inflates run time, utilization, or the hour meter.

  A window now counts as available when its part count reaches
  availability_threshold x target_rate; the new default 0.0 reproduces the old
